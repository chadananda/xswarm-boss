Join the Cool Kids.

All the cool kids are using Omarchy. You've seen them. Flying through their workflow like wizards. No mouse. Pure keyboard magic.

But there's a problem, isn't there?

You're a Mouse Addict

Don't feel bad. We all were. Years of dragging, clicking, scrolling . Your hand reaches for the mouse before you even think about it. It's muscle memory. Bad muscle memory.

You installed Omarchy. You saw the beautiful tiling. You read through the keyboard shortcuts. Super+H? Sure. Super+Shift+J? Okay...

And then you reached for the mouse anyway. Because that's what addicts do.

Reading shortcuts doesn't break addictions. Doing does.

How Games Rewire Your Brain

Here's the thing. Your fingers don't learn from reading. They learn from doing. Under pressure. With stakes.

Guitar Hero taught millions of kids finger patterns . VIM Adventures finally cracked vim for developers who gave up on :help. Duolingo built an empire on making repetition fun.

The pattern is clear: make it a game, and your brain will do the work for free.

Your Omarchy Training Ground

Omarchy Defender is your rehab program for mouse addiction.

Gnome soldiers (yes, that's the joke) are invading your terminal grid . Your only weapons? The exact Omarchy keyboard shortcuts you've been struggling to memorize.

Enemy from the left? Super+H. Spawn a terminal? Super+Enter. Close a window? Super+W.

No time to check the cheat sheet. No time to reach for the mouse. Your fingers figure it out or the Gnomes win. That's how you break an addiction.

Ten Minutes to Join the Cool Kids

Here's the beautiful part. You don't need to "study." You don't need to "practice." You just play.

Ten minutes while your coffee brews . By Friday, Super+H/J/K/L will be automatic. You won't think about it. Your fingers will just know. The mouse will stay on the desk.

You'll finally be one of the cool kids.

The Shortcuts You'll Master

By the time you clear Stage 3, these Omarchy shortcuts will be burned into your muscle memory:

  • Super+H/J/K/L - Navigate windows (vim-style)
  • Super+Enter - Spawn terminal
  • Super+W - Close window
  • Super+Shift+H/J/K/L - Move windows
  • Super+1-9 - Switch workspaces
  • Super+Shift+1-9 - Send to workspace
  • Super+F - Fullscreen
  • Super+T - Toggle floating
  • Super+A - App launcher

Your Choice

Option A: Keep reaching for the mouse like a crutch. Keep watching the cool kids from the sidelines. Maybe someday you'll get the hang of it. Maybe.

Option B: Press Super+Enter above and play for ten minutes. Let your fingers learn while your brain has fun.

The Gnome armada isn't going to defeat itself.
